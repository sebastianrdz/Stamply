# Stamply landing-page assets — v1

Seven transparent PNG marketing mockups generated from the real supplied screenshots. The three businesses are fictional examples. The archive also contains all seven untouched original captures.

## Suggested placement

1. Hero: hero-wallet-showcase.png on a light neutral background. On small screens, use bruma-wallet-phone.png to keep the card readable.
2. How it works: choose how-it-works-enrollment.png or stamp-progress-update.png as the main illustration. Avoid displaying every supporting image simultaneously.
3. Example selector: use bruma-wallet-phone.png, alba-wallet-phone.png and verde-mesa-wallet-phone.png as alternative panels. Keep the associated store images and brand colors from the earlier business kits.
4. Optional proximity illustration: proximity-reminder-phone.png can replace a supporting illustration inside an existing section. It does not require a new section.
5. Pricing, FAQ and closing CTA require no additional imagery.

## Integration

asset-manifest.json provides relative paths, actual image dimensions, section mapping, source filenames and Spanish alt text. Use image containment, preserve aspect ratios, reserve width/height to avoid layout shifts, and lazy-load imagery below the hero. Do not load all example images at full resolution on initial mobile render. Keep words, calls to action and accessible descriptions as live page text.

The images are generated marketing mockups rather than pixel-identical composites. Fine interface details and QR patterns may be redrawn. QR patterns in generated mockups are illustrative and are not verified for scanning. For exact screen content or functional QR behavior, use the untouched original screenshots or a separately supplied working demonstration link/QR.
